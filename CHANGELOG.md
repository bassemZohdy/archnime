### Changed

- **TASK-006** (task): Establish the animated SVG compatibility policy: stages now play in order (the delays were negative and reversed them), stage 1 is the fallback under reduced motion and where animation is stripped, and CSS keyframes are kept as the single format after measuring CSS against SMIL in Chromium, Firefox and WebKit (SMIL cannot honour reduced motion). Sanitising destinations such as Office, Google Docs and the GitHub proxy were not measurable here. Ref: architecture-diagram-renderer/references/documentation-export.md. Validated: `npm test`. Includes: TASK-006.1, TASK-006.2.
